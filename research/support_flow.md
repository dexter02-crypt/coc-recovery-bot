Source: [Player-tag article](https://support.supercell.com/clash-of-clans/en/articles/player-tag.html). date_checked: 2026-11-22. status: observed in indexed extraction.

The player identifier supports friend discovery and account location. It begins with a hash followed by letters, or letters and digits. In Clash of Clans, the player opens the profile at the upper left and finds the identifier beneath its icon. Someone unable to access the village can have an in-game friend inspect that profile. An owner using another account can also inspect a friends list or shared clan roster. The identifier alone is insufficient to recover a village. Instructions for the publisher's other games describe different navigation; they do not alter the Clash of Clans location.

Source: [Account-safety article](https://sc-clashofclans.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/54567895884059-How-Can-I-Keep-My-Account-Safe). date_checked: 2026-11-22. status: observed.

The article recommends Supercell ID, protection and strong email security, including email two-factor authentication. It warns against free-item offers, account sharing, and disclosing personal/account details in chat. Unsolicited requests for login codes can be impersonation. It distinguishes an owner-initiated in-game support conversation from unexpected contact. Another person's recovery request is treated as phishing because support cannot verify that person's permission; the player should contact support personally. Suspected compromise should be reported through official support.
